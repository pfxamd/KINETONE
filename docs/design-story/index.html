NOTHING EXTRA / EVERYTHING INTENTIONAL

Design starts with what it feels like to play.

Before the enclosure, before the colour, before the first sketch comes a simple question: how could this feel more immediate?

Our three imaginary instruments each answer it differently. PULSE gives your rhythm room to breathe. FORM keeps melody within reach. LOOP helps you hear possibilities in sounds you'd normally pass by.

AN OBJECT WITH A POINT OF VIEW

Every detail makes a difference.

A button with a little resistance. A dial you can find without looking. A colour that makes you want to reach out. We treat those details as part of the music.
