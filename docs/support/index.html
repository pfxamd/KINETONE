THE ESSENTIALS

A FEW GOOD
QUESTIONS.

Can I actually play these instruments?

Yes. The Play page includes working browser-based rhythm, synthesis and sampling experiences, powered by the Web Audio API. The physical devices are fictional concepts.

Is KINETONE a real product brand?

No. KINETONE, its devices and their technical specifications are original fictional demonstrations. The website does not sell or ship physical products.

Does the sampler send my microphone recordings to a server?

No. Recording, importing and audio processing all happen in your browser memory. Nothing is uploaded or stored by this website.

Can I use the site without audio?

Yes. All product information and pages are accessible without sound. Audio only starts when you choose to play an instrument.

Why won't my microphone work?

Your browser may require permission, a secure connection or a compatible device. The demo sampler and local audio import work without microphone access.
